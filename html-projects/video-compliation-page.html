My Favorite Anime

I love anime, particularly those featuring the music of composer and music producer Yuki Kajiura. I think she is a genius. Highly underrated, perhaps?

Demon Slayer

This is about a boy whose family has been slaughtered by a demon. Although his sister escapes death, she has been turned into a demon. This anime is about their journey together. Intriguing twists await towards the end of the second season and at the end of it. The clip below is the movie trailer which continues the battle the boy and his friends find themselves in at the end of the second season.

Tsubasa Resrvervoir Chronicles

You either love this anime, or you hate it! It features a group of four people, largely unknown to each other when they first meet, travelling across dimensions to recover the scattered memories of the only female of the group, Sakura. She is the childhood friend of the protagonist Syaoran, who is the one charged and aided by interdimensional witch Yuko to recover Sakura's memories. The clip is a music clip, entitled which is taken from Yuki Kajiura's official YouTube channel as I could not locate an official trailer.

Unlimited Blade Works

This is a fascinating anime taken from the Fate series. The protagonist is an idealistic boy whose desire is to be a hero of justice. He finds himself recruited to take part in a very strange contest. Things get rather complicated when his future self shows up with a view to terminating him. Perhaps with good reason..?
