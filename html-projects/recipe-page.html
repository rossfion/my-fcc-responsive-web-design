Double Chocolate Chip Brownies

I don't have a favorite recipe as such. I just love good, tasty food! However, I do occasionally have a craving for chocolate chip brownies. Since I don't make these from scratch, I tend to "cheat" by using a pre-packaged brownies mix from the local supermarket. When done, it usually disappears within days. I love to have a square or two with a cup of warm milk at bedtime. Don't you? Yummy!

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup of water
  • 1/3 cup of vegetable oil (I sometimes use unrefined coconut oil - for a better taste)
  • 1 egg
  • a dash or two (or more, of cinnamon)

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat oven to 325 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Lightly grease or spray an 8x8-inch baking pan.
  3. Place water, oil and eg in a medium bowl and stir until fully mixed.
  4. Add brownie mix and stir until well blended.
  5. Spread the now fully blended mix in your prepared pan.
  6. Bake for 39-43 minutes.
  7. When baked, allow to cool completely before cutting it up - else you will make a right, royal mess!
